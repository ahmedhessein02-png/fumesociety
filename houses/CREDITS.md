# House logos

Official logos from Wikimedia Commons, converted to black on transparent. Every other house in the catalog has a generated monogram and wordmark from `scripts/make_house_logos.py` (black on transparent). Houses that are not in the catalog are not added. To replace a mark, save `<house slug>.png` here.
