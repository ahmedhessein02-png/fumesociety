Owner credential setup

Choose the username and password you will use to sign in to FumeSociety. This page generates the Cloudflare secret in your browser. It does not send or save your password.

In Cloudflare, select Production, enter FUME_ADMIN as the key, paste this value, check Secret, then choose Add variable and deploy.

Save your username and password in your password manager. The generated value is for Cloudflare only; it is not your login password. This page does not activate the account until you deploy the secret. Close this tab after copying.
